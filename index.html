        How I work

        Data scientist with 4 years of experience building credit risk and customer behaviour models, and the data foundations they run on. Comfortable owning a model end to end, from raw source data through to the decision it informs.

        At AIG I work on production LLM workflows on Palantir Foundry and AIP — AI-assisted claims triage that classifies documents and extracts risk flags across 500K+ claims a year, plus the pipelines, Workshop apps, and alerting that keep it running.
